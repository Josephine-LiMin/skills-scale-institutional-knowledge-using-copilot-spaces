## Summary of Project Management Processes
OctoAcme follows a structured, collaborative approach to project management. The lifecycle spans initiation, planning, execution, risks management, release, and continuous improvement. We rely on distinct roles and personas (like Product Owners, Scrum Masters, Developers, and Stakeholders) to define ownership and ensure alignment throughout the project timeline.
